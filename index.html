NOVA

The marketing site for a fictional team-workflow startup, designed and built to feel like the real thing.

SaaS sites tend to look alike: a gradient, a floating screenshot and three icons in a row. NOVA is drawn like an architect’s set of plans instead, with grid lines, dimension lines and numbered sheets around a product mockup that actually works in the browser.

A portfolio project. NOVA, its customers and its prices are fictional. The whole site, including the app screens, is built in code.

Pasar Malam

An ordering site for a Southeast Asian street-food kitchen. Customers order at the table or for takeaway, then watch their number come up.

Small restaurants often hand online ordering to a delivery app that looks like every other one. Pasar Malam’s own site sells the food with big photography and a look taken from the painted shophouses of Penang and Singapore, and keeps ordering quick.

A portfolio project. Pasar Malam is a fictional restaurant, but ordering, the kitchen board and the demo orders all work. Food photos are from Unsplash.

Aldergrove Hours

A timesheet and payroll system for a 43-person freight company. Staff clock in, managers approve the hours, and HR turns approved time into payslips.

Small companies often run hours through spreadsheets and email: timesheets go missing and overtime gets paid without approval. This app keeps the whole chain in one place, so every figure on a payslip traces back to a clock-in.

A portfolio project. Aldergrove Freight Co. is fictional, but the app, its rules and its three months of demo data all work.
