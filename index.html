Aldergrove Hours

A timesheet and payroll system for a 43-person freight company. Staff clock in, managers approve the hours, and HR turns approved time into payslips.

Small companies often run hours through spreadsheets and email. Timesheets go missing, overtime gets paid without anyone approving it, and payroll is typed up by hand at the end of the month. This app keeps the whole chain in one place, so every figure on a payslip can be traced back to a clock-in.

A portfolio project. Aldergrove Freight Co. is fictional, but the app, its rules and its three months of demo data all work.

From clock-in to payslip

Each step hands a checked record to the next, so nothing is typed twice.

  1. Clock in. Jordan clocks in on his phone. Late starts and breaks are worked out for him.
  2. Timesheet. His week fills itself in from the clock, with time past eight hours flagged as overtime.
  3. Approve. Priya, his manager, approves the week or sends it back with a reason.
  4. Payroll. At month end, HR calculates pay from approved hours and overtime, and is warned about anything still unreviewed.
  5. Payslip. Finalizing locks the timesheets and issues everyone an itemized payslip, with a PDF.

One app, three roles

Everyone signs in to the same app and sees only what their job needs.

Employees

Clock in and out, check the week’s hours, ask for overtime or leave, and read every payslip, on a phone or at a desk.

Managers

See who’s in today, approve timesheets, overtime and leave in one queue, and keep an eye on the team’s hours before they turn into overtime.

HR and payroll

Manage people, salaries and allowances, run monthly payroll, export reports to CSV or Excel, and check the audit log for who changed what.

Built like a real product

The parts nobody sees are the ones that keep payroll right.

Permissions on the server
Employees see only their own pay and managers only their team. Every request is checked, not just hidden in the interface.
Money in whole cents
Pay is calculated in cents and minutes, so nothing drifts by a rounding error. The tax table is a configurable demo, not an official one.
Locked once paid
Finalizing payroll locks the timesheets and overtime it paid for. Late corrections go into the next month.
An audit trail
Every approval, payroll run, salary change and export is logged with who did it and when.
Files people need
Payslips download as PDF. Attendance, overtime and payroll reports export to CSV and Excel.
Tested
43 automated tests cover the payroll maths and the rules behind every screen.
