# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite (user choice). Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS modules, with GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis and Three.js installed from npm. Deployed on Netlify, which uses the existing Netlify Forms contact form.

## Users

Prospective clients who need a website designed and built. The user named "anyone who needs a web design", so the audience is broad: small businesses, founders, creative brands and studios. They arrive from a link or search, judge craft within seconds, and decide whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Fabian Ghani Affandi, a freelance web designer and developer. Its job is to win client enquiries. The site itself is the main proof of skill: its motion, WebGL and typography show what Fabian can build.

## Positioning

A developer who builds the whole site himself, including the design, the motion and the WebGL. The portfolio shows this capability directly instead of describing it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact goes through the Netlify form (`data-netlify="true"`, form name `contact`). It must keep its hidden `form-name` field and client-side validation.
- Dark mode is required, with the choice persisted in `localStorage` under the key `theme`.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Product Principles

1. Show the capability; don't claim it. The site itself is the case study.
2. Stay honest about scope: label concept work as concept work, and show no invented clients.
3. Make first contact effortless. A reachable contact form stays one click away from anywhere on the site.
4. Keep refinement above spectacle. Motion and WebGL must support the content.
